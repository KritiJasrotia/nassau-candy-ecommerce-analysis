# Nassau Candy Distributor – E-commerce Sales & Shipping Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyzes sales data from Nassau Candy Distributor to understand business performance, product profitability, regional sales trends, and recorded shipping times. The goal is to convert raw sales data into meaningful insights that can support business decision-making.

The project was completed as part of my **Data Analysis Internship at Unified Mentor**.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales, gross profit, orders, and units sold.
- Evaluate sales and profitability across regions and product divisions.
- Identify top-performing products and products with lower profit margins.
- Compare yearly and monthly sales performance.
- Analyze recorded shipping times across regions and shipping modes.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard to communicate findings and recommendations.

## Dataset
The dataset contains **10,194 records and 18 original columns**, including:
- Order ID and Order Date
- Ship Date and Ship Mode
- Customer ID
- Region, City, and State/Province
- Division and Product Name
- Sales, Units, Cost, and Gross Profit

Derived columns such as Shipping Days, Profit Margin, Year, Month, and Month Number were created for analysis.

## Project Workflow
1. **Data Inspection:** Examined the dataset structure, column types, missing values, and duplicate records.
2. **Data Preparation:** Converted date columns and created derived analytical columns.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Examined sales, profitability, product performance, regional performance, and recorded shipping patterns.
4. **SQL Analysis:** Used aggregations, grouping, filtering, and sorting to answer business questions.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with KPI cards, slicers, trend charts, and product and regional analysis.
6. **Business Recommendations:** Translated analytical findings into recommendations for monitoring profitability, sales growth, and recorded shipping times.

## Key Performance Indicators
The analysis includes:
- Total Sales
- Total Gross Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Units Sold
- Overall Profit Margin
- Average Recorded Shipping Days

## Key Findings
- **Total Sales:** 141,783.63
- **Total Gross Profit:** 93,442.80
- **Total Orders:** 8,549
- **Total Units Sold:** 38,654
- **Overall Profit Margin:** Approximately 65.91%
- **Sales Growth:** Sales increased by approximately 44.64% in 2025 compared with 2024.
- **Regional Performance:** The Pacific region recorded the highest total sales.
- **Division Performance:** Chocolate contributed the largest share of total sales.
- **Product Profitability:** Product-level analysis highlighted items that may require closer review of pricing and costs.
- **Shipping Analysis:** Recorded shipping times were compared across regions, products, and shipping modes.

## Power BI Dashboard
The interactive dashboard presents:
- KPI cards for overall business performance
- Monthly sales and gross profit trends
- Sales performance by region and division
- Product-level sales and profitability analysis
- Recorded shipping-time analysis
- Key insights and business recommendations

## Business Recommendations
- Monitor low-margin products and review their pricing and cost structures.
- Continue analyzing high-performing regions and products.
- Use monthly sales trends to support demand and inventory planning.
- Investigate unusually high recorded shipping times to identify potential operational improvement opportunities.
- Review performance regularly through the interactive dashboard.

## Project Limitations
The analysis describes patterns in the provided dataset. Relationships between shipping time and profitability should not be interpreted as proof of causation. Shipping-time findings reflect the dates recorded in the dataset.
